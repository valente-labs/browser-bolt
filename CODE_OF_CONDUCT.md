# Code of conduct

Keep Browser Bolt's issues and reviews useful to the people doing the work. Disagree with a claim or implementation by explaining the problem and showing evidence. Give people room to correct mistakes.

These expectations apply in this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces moderated by its maintainers:

- Treat participants respectfully regardless of identity, background, experience, or communication style.
- Do not harass, threaten, insult, target people with discriminatory remarks, or publish sexual content directed at participants.
- Respect privacy. Do not post another person's identifying information, credentials, private messages, or customer data without permission.
- Keep feedback relevant. Do not flood discussions, repeatedly contact someone who has asked you to stop, or retaliate against someone for raising a concern.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock a discussion, or restrict participation to address harmful behavior. They should explain the reason when doing so would not expose private information or prolong harassment. These expectations apply to maintainers too.

To raise a conduct concern about public project content, identify the relevant comment or discussion to a repository maintainer. Share only the minimum context needed. If the report needs private information and no private route is listed, ask for a reporting route before sharing it. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. GitHub's own abuse-reporting process is also available under its platform rules.

This policy governs participation in project spaces. The software remains under its MIT license.
